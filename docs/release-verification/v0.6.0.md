with the downloaded Mac binary and the authorized completion notification.

These results complete the client-evidence release acceptance tracked in
[#94](https://github.com/rebaze/rio/issues/94) and [#101](https://github.com/rebaze/rio/issues/101).

This historical release's portable records are unsigned consistency evidence. Receiver
acknowledgments do not establish ingestion or complete content retention. Broader quality gates,
full-input retention and consumer discovery remain separately scoped. Artifactory validation
was excluded from this release and is tracked in [#88](https://github.com/rebaze/rio/issues/88).
